Cyber Awareness Centre

Common scams targeting Bengaluru residents — stay informed and stay safe.

📱

Fake KYC SMS

Banks never ask for KYC updates via SMS links. Such messages are phishing attempts.

Common in Bengaluru
💸

UPI Payment Fraud

Scammers send payment requests disguised as refunds or cashback. Accepting a UPI request means you are paying, not receiving.

High Risk
📞

Fake Customer Care

Fake bank helpline numbers appear in Google Search. Always use the number printed on your card.

Very Common
🛍️

Online Shopping Fraud

Fake websites clone popular stores. Check for HTTPS, verify the domain, and use official apps.

Medium Risk
👤

Social Media Impersonation

Fake profiles impersonate friends or celebrities to request money or extract information.

Common
💼

Job Scams

Fake work-from-home offers ask for registration fees. Legitimate employers never ask for upfront payment.

Rising
